# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Subscription, growth, and release operators responsible for mobile apps with App Store and Play Store subscription revenue. They use the console during setup, catalogue maintenance, revenue checks, and store-operation troubleshooting.

## Product Purpose

piparo subscription management centralizes app, subscription product, entitlement, offering, subscriber, and store-integration state. Success means teams can connect a store account, map apps, compare product catalogues, import subscription signals, and spot operational issues without switching between App Store Connect, Play Console, spreadsheets, and internal tools.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operator-grade. The UI should feel trustworthy under production pressure: compact, legible, explicit about risk, and clear about what is read-only versus mutating.

## Anti-references

Do not make the tool feel like a marketing dashboard, a decorative analytics landing page, or an opaque automation black box. Avoid generic SaaS hero metrics, ornamental gradients, vague "AI insights", hidden mutations, and broad Admin-key assumptions.

## Design Principles

- Capability-first onboarding: show exactly which store features the connected credential can use.
- Preview before mutation: product and release operations must show diffs before changing local or store data.
- Secrets stay invisible: private keys are one-way uploads, redacted everywhere else, and all secret use is auditable.
- Source-of-truth clarity: distinguish Apple snapshots, local records, raw report imports, and derived metrics.
- Operator density: prefer compact tables, explicit statuses, and actionable errors over decorative summaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast and keyboard access. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Use text labels alongside color-coded status. Keep forms and tables readable for dense operational work.
